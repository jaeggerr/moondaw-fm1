MOONDAW · FM-1 · proposed 2 November 2026 · not built

Help where the hand already is

HELP has a page for every screen and no way to it: one walks back to HOME's root and opens it from the menu, which nobody does in the middle of a bar. This proposes a gesture of its own — OCT− and OCT+ pressed together, from any screen — opening the page of the screen in front, HOME going back to the work rather than out to the menu. And the line under the cursor lighting its controls on the panel: help read with the eyes down on the keys.

None of it is in the firmware. The screens below are real pixels — a patch written to take these pictures, and thrown away. What is kept is the picture and the argument.

The gesture

The one chord the panel had left

Every button on this machine acts on its press — app/ui.h says it plainly: EDIT is the only one that can be held. So no modifier can be pressed before a second button without its own action going off first: HOME + anything has already stepped back, SAVE + anything has already saved.

OCT− and OCT+ are the exception, and by accident. Every screen reads them in the same frame and in opposite directions, so the two together move the keyboard's octave by exactly nothing. The chord would be read in app/app.c before any screen sees the frame, the two buttons rubbed out of it, so even that nothing never happens.

Pressed together, released together. Nothing else on the panel answers to two buttons, so nothing else can be hit by mistake.

The page

A cursor, and the panel says the rest

A page is a block of text one scrolls. It would become a cursor, a line at a time, with a green bar at the left of the line it is on — and the line's controls would light on the machine. Reading stops being a translation: the three keys named under your fingers are the three keys lit under your fingers.

The words are the ones the pages are already written in. Each line is read for whole words the machine knows — the fourteen buttons, and OP1 to OP6 and MONO, the black keys EDIT gives a command to — so a page lights itself and nothing is written twice. SELECT is not SEL: whole words only.

The three black keys light where the hand plays them. A knob has no LED, so what KNOB 3 does is only ever read.

PLAY and REC are never taken: their LEDs are their state, and a help that lied about what is playing would be worse than none.

To take out

The cursor that moves by itself

HELP opens with its cursor already on the topic of the last screen worked on — a help of where you were, for a screen nobody reaches. Read from the menu, with no memory of why, it looks like a fault: the list opens on PATTERN and the line reads “white: where you came from”, explaining a cleverness that cost a walk back to the root. It should go. The list opens on its first topic and says nothing, because the page you actually want is one chord away from the screen you are on.

What it gains

Nothing to explain. A list that opens where lists open.

What it costs

Through the menu, the topic is found by turning. Four turns at most, and the menu is no longer the way in.

Left open

The pages are still flat

  • SONG's page is forty-four lines. A cursor makes them walkable, not scannable. What they want is a grouping — the cursor's own gestures, then the commands, then the way out — under a grey intertitle. That is a content job, ten pages of it, and no part of it was tried here.
  • A carousel was considered and dropped. One page at a time hides everything you did not ask for, and the thing you look up in a hurry is never the slide you are on.
  • EFFECTS does not light anything. Its lines are effect names, not controls.
  • A line that names PLAY cannot light it. PLAY's LED belongs to the transport.
