MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

The home screen, and the list of songs

What replaces the temporary playback screen. A single column: at the top the current song, to pick up again, then the songs kept on the device, then the cell that creates one. SELECT moves down the list; the buttons at the bottom say what you can do with the chosen line. Nothing to turn here, so no cards.

Frame 1 · at power-on

The cursor is on what you pick up again

The machine opens on the song it has in memory, already loaded: OPEN (the EDIT button) goes back to it without reading anything again. At the root there is nothing above, so the logo takes the place of the back label, as in the app. On the right of the bar, the space used in flash.

What you see

The current song and 10 lines of list. Beyond that, the list scrolls.

What the chrome takes

30 px at the top (bar, left-hand manual), 25 at the bottom (the twelve buttons).

What is missing

HELP and SETTINGS, which the app has on its home screen. They do not exist on the FM-1 yet.

Frames 2 and 3 · in the list

Open, listen, delete

On a song in the list, three buttons light up. PLAY lets you hear it without opening it. DELETE does not delete: it asks the question on the line, in red, and a second press deletes; any other command cancels. The FM-1's knobs have no push switch and there is no B+A combination, so the guard is the second press.

Already settled

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/menu.c): one column, SELECT, OPEN on the EDIT button, DELETE guarded by a second press, the SAVE / DISCARD question when the current song has changes, + NEW that creates SONG nn and opens it, the machine that comes back to the song that was open.
  • Departure from the drawing: PLAY only lights up on the current song. Listening to a song in the list without opening it would need a second song in memory, and there is only room for one. To hear it, you open it.
  • The demo song is not a line in the list: it is what you find when the flash is empty. Once saved, it becomes a song like any other.
  • OPEN leads to PATTERN, on the first block the first phrase plays, as long as SONG and PHRASE do not exist.

What I propose, and why

  • One column, not two. The app puts its entries on the left and a pane on the right; in 240 px, 12-character names would no longer fit next to anything else.
  • OPEN is on the EDIT button, and DELETE where PATTERN has CLEAR: the same place for the same kind of gesture from one screen to the next. PLAY never moves.
  • + NEW creates and opens an empty song that names itself (SONG 01, SONG 02…), as NEW in the app opens what it has just made.
  • Opening another song when the current one has changes asks the app's question, the same way as DELETE: the line says "KIDS HAS CHANGES", and two buttons light up, SAVE in its place and DISCARD in place of DELETE. Nothing saves itself.
  • A cell with no word is a button that does nothing on this screen: it keeps its place so that the two rows stay the map of the buttons.
  • The size of each song and the space used are shown: 292 KB for everything, it is a resource you must see.

What is still open

  • Renaming. It means typing letters without a letter keyboard. Felucca does it on the white keys, phone-style; I have not drawn it. Until then, a song keeps the name it had on the computer, or SONG nn.
  • Bringing a song in from the computer. No path exists yet (the firmware's USB can only update itself). That is what will fill this list.
