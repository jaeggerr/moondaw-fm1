MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

Pick, copy, paste

Copy a bar again, move a chord, put the same effect on ten steps. In the app, SELECT is a mode and a tap sets the second corner of the rectangle. Here the second corner is the cursor: the two left knobs carry it, as always. On PATTERN only, to begin with.

Frame 1 · the mode on, the rectangle extended

SELECT plants one corner, the left hand carries the other

One press on SELECT (the EDIT button): the cursor's cell becomes the fixed corner, the button lights up in cyan. SELECT and ALGORITHM move the cursor as before, and everything between the corner and the cursor is taken, in amber. Three buttons change their word while the mode is on: COPY, PASTE, and CLEAR, which empties the whole rectangle. A second press on SELECT lets go of everything.

What is taken

The covered fields, not whole steps: here N1 to N3 of six steps, without their instrument or their effects.

What goes quiet

The four right knobs and the keyboard: in the mode, you take, you do not write.

What is shown

The size in the bar: SEL 3×6, fields × steps.

Frame 2 · paste

PASTE pastes where the cursor is

COPY keeps the rectangle lit (you can follow with CLEAR to move it). To paste: go where you want, SELECT, PASTE. What was copied lands from the cursor's cell, downwards, and the mode turns off. PASTE is off as long as nothing has been copied.

Above: a single effect copied (GAT 03), the cursor on the FX2 of step 08, SELECT just pressed. PASTE is lit; it will put GAT 03 in that slot without touching the step's notes.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/pattern.c, the clipboard in app/edit.c): the mode, the rectangle, COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, and the app's paste rules.
  • CLEAR turns the mode off, like PASTE. COPY leaves it on.
  • Changing block with PRESETS turns the mode off: what was taken belonged to the other block.
  • The bar says SEL 2x4 with a lowercase x: the font has no × sign.
  • What PASTE could not write is not reported (steps lost at the end of the block, a refused effect). The app says so in its status line; here there is none.

What I propose, and why

  • The paste rules are the app's. Only the copied fields are written, the rest of the target does not move. What runs past the end of the block is lost; nothing wraps to the start. An effect that would say the same thing twice on one step is refused.
  • An effect copied alone lands in the FX slot under the cursor, whatever slot it came from.
  • The cursor on the step number takes the whole step: notes, instrument, effects.
  • What is copied survives a change of block, track and phrase: that is how you copy a bar somewhere else. It is forgotten when another song is opened.
  • A paste or a clear is undone with a single UNDO.
  • No CUT: COPY then CLEAR, two presses, the rectangle stays lit between the two.
  • In the mode, LOOP and INSTR no longer exist: their buttons say COPY and PASTE. You leave the mode to get them back.

What is still open

  • PHRASE: its EDIT button says OPEN, and there is no room for SELECT without moving something. I propose nothing until selection has proved itself on PATTERN.
  • Transposing a selection with a knob (raising three steps by an octave): tempting, since the right knobs are free in the mode. Not in the app, so not here.
