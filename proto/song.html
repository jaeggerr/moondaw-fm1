MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

The song, phrase by phrase

The arrangement: one line per phrase, one column per track, in each cell the number of the block the track plays. The eight tracks fit side by side, which you cannot do on PATTERN. It is the screen OPEN leads to from the home screen, and from which you go down to a phrase or a block.

Frame 1 · the cursor on a cell

SELECT the phrase, ALGORITHM the track

The same hands as on PATTERN. KNOB 1 changes the cell's block: the blocks of this track, then ON (plays nothing and cuts nothing), then the empty cell. KNOB 2 sets the cell's level. The other cells in the column that play the same block get a pale green background. OPEN opens the block in PATTERN.

What you see

10 phrases of the 19 in KIDS, and the 8 tracks.

What the chrome takes

82 px at the top (bar, cards, manual, song line, header), 25 at the bottom.

What is missing

The length of each phrase: it has no column, it is on a card.

Frames 2 and 3 · the whole phrase, and the song

All the way left, and all the way up

ALGORITHM fully left puts the cursor on the phrase number: the buttons then act on the phrase (open it, insert one, delete it) and KNOB 1 sets its length. SELECT fully up puts the cursor on the song line: the four cards become the tempo, the swing, the subdivision and the number of tracks.

Already settled

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/songview.c): the grid, the two hands, the song line, the phrase number, the cards, + PHRASE, deletion on a second press, PLAY from the cursor's phrase, SAVE, and what the device does not carry in the bar.
  • A lowered cell shows it: an amber line at the bottom of the cell, as wide as its level.
  • A reference is shown on its line (REF 01) and is not edited here. Deleting the phrase it points to turns it into an empty phrase.
  • OPEN on a phrase number opens PHRASE, and MIXER opens MIXER (written on 10/10). MUTE and SOLO are on MIXER, MUTE also on PHRASE.
  • UNDO is written (10/10): it also brings back a deleted phrase.
  • Not written yet, so unlit: LOOP, REC.

What I propose, and why

  • The buttons keep their place: OPEN on EDIT, CLEAR on LFO, LOOP, MIXER, SAVE, UNDO, PLAY, REC where PATTERN has them. HOME says ‹ MENU. The cell that said SONG in PATTERN (the SEQ button) becomes + PHRASE: you are already there.
  • PLAY plays the song from the cursor's phrase.
  • What the FM-1 does not carry is written in the bar, in amber: KIDS has 18 lines of samples, zones and amp settings that the device ignores, and that a save from here would lose. It is the information the old screen gave and that had disappeared.
  • CLEAR on a phrase number deletes the phrase, with the second press of the home screen. On a cell, it empties it in one go.
  • A new block: turning KNOB 1 past the track's last written block gives the first free number, empty. No extra button.

What is still open

  • Sections, titles and references. The model carries them and the player plays them, but none of your 13 songs contains any: I had nothing real to draw, so I did not draw them. I propose to show them as in the app (an amber title above the group, a reference that carries its target's name) and not to edit them on the FM-1 at first: a title means typing letters.
  • A cell's level when it is not full: no song has one either. A mark in the cell, or only the card?
  • The time signature (steps per beat, beats per bar) has no card: there are only four.
