MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

One sound, four knobs at once

An instrument's settings arranged in rows of four, because the right hand has four knobs. SELECT picks the row, and the four cards become its four settings: the whole envelope under the fingers, then the whole filter. Each cell is aligned under the card, so under the knob, that sets it. The keyboard plays the sound.

Frame 1 · the envelope

Attack, decay, sustain, release

The chosen row is in green and the cards spell out its four settings, with their value translated (milliseconds, a percentage). The curve above draws the envelope and moves with the knobs. You get here with the INSTR button of PATTERN, on the instrument of the step under the cursor; PRESETS moves to the neighbouring instrument.

What you see

The thirteen settings the FM-1 carries, and the four layers, without scrolling.

What the chrome takes

59 px at the top, 25 at the bottom, 38 for the curve.

What is missing

Drive, amp, tone and sample zones: the FM-1 does not have them.

Frames 2 and 3 · the sound, and the layers

The engine at the top, the layers at the bottom

The first row carries the engine, its timbre and the two levels. The last four are the layers: a layer fits on one row because its engine and its timbre are chosen on a single knob (PULSE 25%, WAVE SAW, NOISE SHORT… or nothing). A dotted row is a free layer: turning KNOB 1 creates it.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/instrument.c): the rows of four, the envelope curve, layers created and removed with the knob, the keyboard that plays the sound, PRESETS, SAVE, PLAY.
  • Departure from the drawing: the cards do not translate the values yet (the milliseconds, the sustain percentage). They show the number from the file; the total length of the envelope is written on the curve.
  • An instrument that is silent here (SMPL, VOX, PLUCK) says so in the bar, in amber, and its engine is in amber in its cell. One notch of KNOB 1 gives it an FM-1 engine.
  • UNDO added on 10/10, in the place it has everywhere (the ARP button): it was not in the drawing.
  • The same note preview serves PATTERN: a note you write there with the keyboard is heard.

What I propose, and why

  • Four per row, always. The envelope has four settings and so does the filter: the split comes by itself, and the order of the cells is the order of the knobs.
  • The keyboard plays the instrument, at the pitch of the keys, as long as you are on this screen. OCT− and OCT+ shift the keyboard as elsewhere.
  • PLAY plays the phrase you came from, to set the sound within the song.
  • The values are the file's (0 to 255) in the cells, and their translation on the card: that is what you read in a .trk.
  • Few buttons: the way back, SAVE and PLAY. Setting a sound needs nothing else.

What remains open

  • The name. Shown, not editable: still the question of letters.
  • Does changing engine erase anything? In the app, no: the timbre is simply brought back within the new engine's bounds. I keep that.
  • Instruments on SMPL, VOX or PLUCK: I propose showing them with their engine in amber and a note that they are silent here, and letting KNOB 1 move them to an FM-1 engine if you want.
  • A sound library (the app's presets): nothing planned on the device.
  • The filter has no curve, only the envelope. A second curve when the cursor is on the filter row?
