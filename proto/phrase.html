The phrase, all eight tracks at once

What SONG does not show (the notes) and what PATTERN does not show (the other tracks): one step per line, one column per track, and in each cell the first note of the step. This is the screen where you see what falls together. You get here with OPEN on a phrase number in SONG; it is the last screen missing.

Frame 1 · the cursor on a cell

SELECT the step, ALGORITHM the track, PRESETS the phrase

The same hands as elsewhere. The keyboard writes the cell's note and plays it, as on PATTERN; KNOB 1 moves it up or down a semitone, KNOB 2 an octave. OPEN goes down into PATTERN, on this track and this step. PRESETS moves to the next or previous phrase without moving the cursor.

What you see

10 steps of the phrase's 64, and the 8 tracks.

What the cell says

The first note. One cyan dot per extra note in the chord. Without a note: the first effect, in amber.

What is missing

The other notes of the chord, the instrument, the effects of a step that has a note: they are in PATTERN.

Frames 2 and 3 · the column head, and playback

At the very top: the track

SELECT all the way up puts the cursor on the column head: the track and the number of its block. From there, OPEN opens the block at its first step. MUTE cuts the track, from the head as from any cell of the column; a muted track keeps its notes, in grey. PLAY loops the phrase and the page follows the playhead.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/phrase.c): the grid, the dot per extra note, the column head, the two knobs, the keyboard that writes and plays, CLEAR, MUTE, OPEN, MIXER, PRESETS, SAVE, PLAY.
  • OPEN from PHRASE, then HOME in PATTERN, returns to PHRASE: PATTERN's way back says ‹ PHR. Its SONG button always leads to SONG.
  • The page follows playback until the hand turns SELECT: the hand then takes over the page, and hands it back at the next PLAY. It moves a whole page at a time, never line by line.
  • The keyboard makes chords as on PATTERN: the first key writes the first note, the keys pressed while holding it write the next ones.
  • The left manual says PRESETS PHR and not PHRASE, to leave room for the keyboard position (OCT F3-G5), which I had forgotten in the drawing.
  • MUTE when lit is red, as on MIXER, not cyan as in the drawing.
  • A reference does not open: it has no notes of its own, and PRESETS skips it.

What I propose, and why

  • One dot per extra note, not the notes themselves. In the app, N2 to N4 stack up very small to the right of N1. Here the smallest readable font is 8 px and the cell 23: there is no room. The dot says "chord"; the notes are one OPEN away.
  • The buttons keep their place: CLEAR, OPEN, MIXER, SAVE, PLAY where PATTERN and SONG have them. HOME says ‹ SONG. MUTE is where it is on MIXER.
  • SOLO is not here: its place on MIXER is CLEAR's, which I keep. It stays on MIXER, one button away.
  • CLEAR empties the cell in one go (the step's notes and effects), as on PATTERN.
  • A track with no block in this phrase draws nothing: an empty column, and the cursor crosses it without stopping.
  • A block shorter than the phrase repeats (T1 is 16 steps, the phrase 64): the grid shows what plays, and writing at step 14 writes step 04 of the block.

What remains open

  • REC: not written elsewhere either, so off here too. UNDO and LOOP are written (10/10).
  • The phrase name: in the app, this is where a phrase is named. With no letters on the device, the name shows in the bar when it exists and cannot be edited.
  • Selection (copy, paste): not before it exists on PATTERN.
