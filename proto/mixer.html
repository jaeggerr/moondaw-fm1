MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

Eight tracks, four settings each

A track has four things to say about its place in the mix: its volume, its pan, what it sends to the reverb and what it sends to the delay. Four settings, four knobs: SELECT picks the track, and the right hand has the whole track under its fingers. The first row sets the reverb and the delay themselves.

Frame 1 · the cursor on a track

One row per track

Each cell carries its value and a line that draws it: the length for a level, the position for the pan. The track number is in the first cell. You get here with the MIXER button of SONG or PATTERN, and the way back returns to where you came from.

What you see

The eight tracks and the two returns, without scrolling.

What the chrome takes

59 px at the top, 25 at the bottom.

What is missing

Levels that move during playback, and the metronome.

Frames 2 and 3 · the returns, and muting a track

The room and the echo at the top, MUTE and SOLO at the bottom

At the very top, the returns row: the size and damping of the reverb, the time and feedback of the delay. Two buttons act on the track under the cursor: MUTE cuts it, SOLO leaves it on its own. A muted track has its number in red, a soloed track in green, and this is not saved in the song, as in the app.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/mixer.c): one row per track, the two returns in the first row, MUTE and SOLO on the track under the cursor, SAVE, PLAY, and the way back to the screen you came from (SONG or PATTERN).
  • SOLO writes nothing into the song. It silences the other tracks without muting them: when you release it, the mutes are as you left them.
  • Pan goes from L64 to R63, as in the file: hard right, one sixty-fourth of the track stays on the left.
  • On the returns row, MUTE and SOLO do nothing and are off.
  • UNDO is written (10/10): one press undoes one gesture; it is off when there is nothing to undo.
  • Not there yet: the VU meters, the metronome.

What I propose, and why

  • The track as a row, not a vertical strip. A mixing desk draws strips; here the four settings of a track must fall under the four knobs, which are in a line.
  • Volume goes from 0 to 127 and is linear, as in the file: 64 is half the amplitude. Pan is written L18, C, R14.
  • MUTE and SOLO live here. That was the question left open on SONG.
  • PLAY plays the song from the phrase you came from.
  • A send at zero is written in grey: you see at a glance what sends where.

What remains open

  • The VU meters. The app shows each track's level during playback. The FM-1 engine does not measure it yet; it costs a little computation per track.
  • The metronome is on this screen in the app. It does not exist in the FM-1 engine.
  • The master volume is the device's MASTER knob and appears nowhere on the screen. Should it be shown here?
