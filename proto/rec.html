MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

REC: play, and it is written

What you decided for the app on 3 September, applied to a machine that has a real keyboard: one press, a four-beat count-in, the phrase starts, and what you play on the 27 keys is written into the block, snapped to the nearest step. Nothing finer is written on its own. On PATTERN to begin with.

Frame 1 · the count-in

One press on REC

The button turns red, the bar counts the four beats (REC 4 … REC 1) and a click marks them. Nothing plays yet, nothing is written. A second press during the count-in cancels.

Frame 2 · the take

The phrase runs, the notes appear

On the fourth beat the phrase starts from the top and loops, the click goes on marking the beats. Each key is heard at once and is written to the step nearest the moment it is pressed; several keys on the same step make a chord. The page follows the play head. REC a second time stops the take and keeps playing, to hear the result; STOP stops everything.

What you see

The notes of this take on a pale red ground until it stops, the playing row in green.

What you hear

The phrase, the click, and the key played — one at a time: keyboard monitoring has only one voice.

What goes quiet

The knobs, SELECT, CLEAR, FX, INSTR: during the take you play.

What I propose, and why

  • A key that lands on a step already written replaces it (its notes, not its instrument or its effects). Steps you do not play again keep what they had: that is how you fill a phrase pass after pass.
  • Releasing writes OFF at the nearest step, if that step is empty and no other key is held. Without it, every take would be legato and a played silence would be lost. This is the point I am asking you to decide.
  • With a loop set (LOOP), the take runs inside the loop: you record two bars without waiting for the rest of the phrase.
  • The instrument is not written: the notes take the one in effect at that point of the block, like a note written by hand.
  • A single UNDO takes back the whole take.
  • The click is not in the song: it exists only during the count-in and the take.

What is still open

  • PHRASE: the same button could record there, on the cursor's track. After PATTERN.
  • The click during plain playback (a metronome, outside REC): not proposed here.
  • Hearing chords while playing: it would take several monitoring voices. The chords are written correctly; during the take only their last note is heard.
