MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026

The loop, in three presses

Loop a few steps of a block or a phrase while you work on them. In the app it is a switch and a START / END bar where you tap a row; here there is nothing to tap, so a single button, where the cursor is. Never saved: it is a listening tool.

Frames 1 and 2 · PATTERN

LOOP sets the start, END sets the end

First press on LOOP: the cursor's step becomes the start, its number turns cyan and the button says END. Move down with SELECT, second press: the end is set, the loop exists, the LOOP button stays lit in cyan. Third press: no more loop. If the end is set above the start, the two swap.

What you see

The gutter left of the steps and their numbers in cyan, and the range in the bar.

What you hear

PLAY plays the range in a loop. Set during playback, it takes effect at once.

What does not change

The song: nothing is written, SAVE keeps none of it, UNDO does not see it.

Frame 3 · PHRASE

The same loop, seen on the eight tracks

On PHRASE the button and the three presses are the same. It is one single loop: set on PHRASE, it is still there when you go down into PATTERN with OPEN, and when you come back. It is cleared when you change phrase or go back to SONG.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — approved and written (app/loop.c, the range in core/player.c): the three presses, the gutter, the range in the bar, the same loop on PATTERN and PHRASE.
  • On a PHRASE column head, LOOP sets nothing (it is not a step); there it can only clear a loop that is set.
  • The range gives up its place in the bar to SAVED or POOL FULL while they are shown.
  • MIXER and SONG play the song: the loop has no effect there. PLAY from INSTRUMENT plays the phrase, so the loop.

What I propose, and why

  • A button instead of a bar. The app's START / END bar is there to say which of the two ends the next tap sets. Here the button says it itself by changing its word.
  • No memory of the ends. In the app, switching off keeps START and END for later. Here the third press forgets them: setting them again costs two presses.
  • Changing one end: clear and set again. There is no gesture to move a single end.
  • In PATTERN, a block shorter than its phrase: the loop covers the first pass of the block. It is set in phrase steps, and the steps of a block are those of its first pass.
  • On SONG, LOOP stays off for now: in the app it means something else there (looping the phrase instead of following the song). That would be another mockup.

What is still open

  • A loop across two phrases (to hear a transition): it exists in the app; I do not propose it here until this one has been used.
