MOONDAW · FM-1 · mockup of 10 October 2026, revised

Pattern, no D-pad, no finger

The app's PATTERN screen, re-measured for a 240 × 240 display and for what the FM-1 has under the hands: four knobs left of the screen, four on the right, twelve buttons and a 27-key keyboard. The drawing stays the iPhone's — slabs, nine slots per step, cyan where you are, green for the cursor. It is not an 8 × 8 character grid: the FM-1's screen paints whatever you want, and Felucca already draws anti-aliased fonts on it.

Frame 1 · the cursor on a note

Two hands, two roles

The left hand moves: SELECT goes up and down by one step, ALGORITHM goes from one field to the next, PRESETS changes block. The right hand writes: the four knobs KNOB 1–4 are the four cards under the bar, in the same order, and only speak of the field under the cursor. A key on the keyboard writes its note in the slot and plays it.

What you see

11 whole steps. The iPhone shows 8, the console 17.

What the chrome takes

66 px at the top (bar, cards, left-hand manual, header), 25 at the bottom (the twelve buttons).

What is missing

The Edit / Listen rails. Their role goes to the twelve buttons, drawn at the bottom in their places.

Frame 2 · the cursor on an effect

Turning KNOB 1 opens the list

On an effect slot, the first card picks the command and the list opens beside the slot while the knob turns — the same alphabetical list as everywhere, with --- at the top to clear. The second card sets the value; for VIB, TRM and SCP there are two cards, one per nibble.

Frame 3 · playing, with a selection made

What plays in green, what is taken in amber

PLAY plays the phrase. The playing row gets the app's background and green bar; the cursor stays where the hand left it. SELECT (the EDIT button) is a mode: when it is on, the two left knobs extend the rectangle instead of moving the cursor, and three buttons change their word.

The panel

Who does what on this screen

The screen here is at half the mockup's size: on a laptop with a dense screen, that is roughly its real size if the display is 1.3 inches — I do not know its diagonal; neither Felucca nor SLOOP gives it. It is the first thing to check when the device arrives: it decides the height of a row.

27 keys, from F to G. On a note slot: writes the note and plays it; several keys held together fill N1 to N4. OCT− and OCT+ shift the keyboard by one octave, and the top bar says where it is.

green: the word printed on the device already names the function amber: the printed word says something else

Four buttons out of twelve do something other than what is printed on them (SEL, ENV, LFO, ARP). That is why the screen redraws the twelve buttons in their places, two rows of six: you read the position, not the label. The words change from one screen to the next, the places never do.

Already decided

  • 10/10 — the effect list is written (frame 2). It opens to the left of the slot as soon as KNOB 1 turns on it, stays a second and a half after the last detent, and closes on any other gesture. Six effects around the current one, each with the app's description line, in 8 px like the rest (the drawing showed them smaller): the panel is 124 px wide instead of 112.
  • 10/10 — the left hand moves, the right hand writes. SELECT the step, ALGORITHM the field, PRESETS the block; KNOB 1–4 the four cards.
  • 10/10 — the twelve buttons: FX, LOOP, INSTR, CLEAR, SELECT, MIXER on the top row; ‹ PHR, SAVE, UNDO, SONG, PLAY, REC on the bottom one. Redrawn on the screen in their places.
  • 10/10 — the button words in 9 px bold, and an off button in light grey (COL_BEAT): in 8 px semibold they were unreadable in the simulator.
  • 10/10 — the monospace at 8 px, without anti-aliasing. The 6 px labels were too small and blurry. On the device and in the simulator, JetBrains Mono is drawn on whole pixels at 8 px: sharp, and no step lost. This page cannot show it; a browser always anti-aliases. The bold condensed stays anti-aliased. ALGORITHM is written ALGO to fit on the line.
  • 10/10 — back leads to SONG as long as PHRASE does not exist: the bar says ‹ SONG and the phrase you came from is written beside it (P02). The SONG button leads there too.
  • 10/10 — the cards are at the top, under the bar, level with the knobs they name. The twelve buttons stay at the bottom.

What I propose, and why

  • Nine slots per step, as everywhere. The step, N1 to N4, IN, FX1 to FX3, full or dotted, with the two cyan seams after N4 and after IN. They fit in 240 px with notes of 10.5 px in bold condensed.
  • Clearing is turning all the way down. The FM-1's knobs do not push. Below the lowest note there is OFF then ---; the effect list starts with ---; the instrument with --. CLEAR empties the slot in one go.
  • The keyboard is a real keyboard, so it writes notes. The rule "no chromatic keyboard" was aimed at a keyboard drawn on glass. Here it is under the fingers, and it is the best thing the machine has. KNOB 1 and 2 keep the semitone and the octave for touch-ups.
  • The range of an RMP stays in the right margin, in dark amber, on the steps it covers.
  • A card that is off keeps its place and says it does nothing on this field.

What is still open

  • The role of REC. I left it able to light up without making it do anything: recording on the fly the keys played during playback, quantised to the step, would be its logical next step.
  • Redo has no button. A held UNDO, or a thirteenth word somewhere. UNDO itself is written (10/10): one press takes back one gesture — everything one hand wrote in the same place without pausing for more than a second.
  • MUTE and SOLO are not on this screen. In the app they are on PHRASE.
  • Changing track from PATTERN: no knob is free. In the app it has not been possible either since 23/09; you go through PHRASE.
  • The technical prerequisite. This drawing needs anti-aliased fonts in the firmware, in place of the text grid I wrote for the playback screen. Felucca does it with fonts precomputed at compile time; Barlow Condensed and JetBrains Mono are under a free licence.
