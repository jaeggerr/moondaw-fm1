By place on a US keyboard. Buttons: 1–6 are FX SCL ENV LFO EDIT GLO and Q W E R T Y are HOME SAVE ARP SEQ PLAY REC, two rows of six as on the machine; A and ' are OCT− and OCT+; space is PLAY too. Notes: Z S X D C F V B H N J M , L . ; / then 7 U I 9 O 0 P [ = ]. Encoders: wheel over a knob; the arrows are SELECT (up, down) and ALGORITHM (left, right).
